# Index freshness
Search results are only as current as the index behind them. Full rebuilds are simple and correct but expensive, so most systems blend a periodic rebuild with incremental updates for documents that changed recently. The tradeoff is not just cost. A freshly updated document can rank worse than it did before because its term statistics have changed while the rest of the collection has not, a problem known as index churn. Serving a slightly stale result is often better than serving an inconsistent one, which is why some stacks hold a document out of the index until the update settles.
