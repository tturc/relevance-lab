# Tokenization and stemming
Tokenization decides what counts as a word before anything else happens. Splitting on non alphanumeric characters is the usual starting point, then lowercasing so case does not split a term. Stemming reduces inflections to a common root so that queries, query, and querying all match, at the cost of merging words that should stay apart. A full Porter stemmer has hundreds of rules and still misfires on technical vocabulary, so many systems use a lighter suffix rule and accept the misses. The rule to keep: index and query must use the identical tokenizer, or terms silently fail to meet.
